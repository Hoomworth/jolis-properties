Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about properties, documentation, and the purchase process.

Are all your properties free from government acquisition?

Yes, Jolis Properties conducts rigorous legal checks on all listings. We only list and market properties that are 100% free from government acquisition and have secure, verifiable titles.

Do you offer flexible payment plans?

Absolutely. Most of our land developments and luxury apartments offer flexible payment options stretching from 3 to 12 months with a reasonable initial down payment.

What legal documents do I get upon final payment?

Depending on the estate, you will be issued a physical Allocation Letter, a Deed of Assignment, and a Registered Survey Plan in your name upon full payment.

Can I visit the property site before making a payment?

Yes, we conduct free property inspections from Monday through Saturday. Contact our team to book a convenient date and time.
